// OPERATION SEQUENCE

THE CRAFTSMANSHIP PROCESS

01 / PARADIGM PHASE

CAD Architectural Design

We convert raw conceptual visions into comprehensive 3D CAD parameter blueprints, assessing weight distributions and load structural configurations.

02 / DECONSTRUCTION

CNC High-Power Laser Cutting

Raw sheets are positioned into modern multi-kilowatt fiber lasers, slicing alloys clean with pristine high-speed precision cutting tolerances.

03 / THERMAL FUSION

Precision Arc & TIG Welding

Certified high-grade smiths fuse structural segments under inert shielding gas chambers to achieve unbreakable, sleek load-tested seam joints.

04 / SURFACE DEFENSE

Electrostatic Powder Coating

The final treated product receives polymer resin powders baked at 200°C to harden into a premium weather-shielding coat armored for decades.
