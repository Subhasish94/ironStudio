FAQ MATRIX NETWORK

Technical reference specifications on materials, system integrations, and finishing processes.

Our multiaxially balanced fiber lasers hold structural clean cuts to within ±0.125 millimeters. This dynamic control layout prevents micro-fracturing along high-strength material sections up to 30mm thick while limiting heat-affected zones.
Every internal drive system includes an dry-contact control card compatible with Wiegand, RS-485, and network IP relays. Core mechanical layouts use industrial brushless motors paired with optical safety encoders and torque-sensing stop profiles.
Finished items are grit blasted to white-metal specifications before resin processing. Applying an organic zinc base layer beneath thick structural polyester gives field life expectancy markers above 15+ years against weather erosion and ultraviolet fade.
